# Thiago Bertoldi

[![Design. Build. Question. — Human–AI workflows, infrastructure, and observability.](assets/profile-banner.svg?v=studio-slate)](https://thiagobertoldi.com)

I'm an **Expert Software Engineer at SUSE AI** and an **MSc student in Knowledge Engineering and Management at UFSC**, based in Florianópolis, Brazil.

I design workflows for **people and AI agents**, and build the infrastructure that puts AI to work. I'm interested in what surrounds the model: clear responsibilities, useful context, deliberate architecture, and evidence to decide what happens next.

At SUSE, I contribute to **AI Factory**, from workload deployment and Fleet integration to private application sources, and lead development of the **AI Observability Extension**. My background spans embedded Linux and IoT, data and ML platforms, and technical leadership.

I've led engineering teams. Today, I also design and direct AI-assisted workflows. Defining the work, providing context, and reviewing the result keep turning out to matter.

## Research and questions

My master's research explores how software-task specifications and organizational experience can guide the selection and adaptation of coding-agent models and harnesses—the tools, instructions, context, and controls around a model.

I also care about open source, digital sovereignty, software architecture, and how people keep their judgment when working with AI.
